# What is Spelling Creator?

Spelling Creator is an open-source tool for writing, sharing and running lessons for
**Spelling**: the ways nonspeaking people communicate by pointing to letters on
a letterboard or keyboard. You write a passage in sections, add pictures,
spelling words and questions, then print it, export it to Word or Google Docs,
or run it on screen with your speller. Lessons can be shared on a public hub
where other people can copy and adapt them.

## Spelling is not one method

"Spelling" is an umbrella term, and it is easy to mix up with the names of
particular methods. A few you will come across:

- **[Spelling to Communicate (S2C)](https://i-asc.org/)**, overseen by I-ASC
  (the International Association for Spelling as Communication).
- **Rapid Prompting Method (RPM)**.
- **[Spellers Method](https://www.spellers.com/)**.

Many practitioners, parents and communication partners don't follow any one of
these by name, and mix ideas from several. There is no single standard for how
a lesson should look, how long answers should be, or how questions are marked.

Spelling Creator tries to fit all of this. Some of its defaults come from a
particular source (treating the two "semi-open" question types as one family,
for example, follows the S2C guidebook), and the guide says so where that's the
case. The rest, like the question colors and the usual lesson shape, are simply
this app's choices. Treat every default as
a starting point. The lesson checks and the AI helpers make suggestions, not
rules, and you can change or ignore anything to suit how you and your speller
work.
